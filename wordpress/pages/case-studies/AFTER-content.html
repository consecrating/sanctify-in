Featured Case Study: Counto Motors, Mercedes-Benz Goa

The brief

Counto Motors is the only Mercedes-Benz passenger vehicle dealership in Goa, operating from Ribandar. In a market where buyers research extensively online before ever visiting a showroom, the dealership needed a digital presence that matched the standard of the marque and could be found by high-intent local buyers.

What we did

  • Built a dealership website positioning the showroom for luxury vehicle buyers in Goa
  • Structured content around the searches buyers actually use, such as Mercedes-Benz dealer and luxury car showroom in Goa
  • Established consistent business listings across regional directories and classified platforms to reinforce local search signals
  • Maintained name, address and phone consistency across every listing so search engines resolve the dealership to a single verified entity

Why it worked

Luxury automotive purchases are researched, not impulsive. By making the dealership consistently discoverable at the point of research and presenting it with the polish the brand demands, the digital presence supported the showroom rather than competing with it. Read the full project write-up on the Counto Motors project page.

Featured Case Study: Kenkre Dental Care, Panjim

The brief

Kenkre Dental Care is a dental clinic in Panjim led by Dr. Shilpa Kenkre Kamat, offering cosmetic dentistry, veneers, bonding, tooth whitening, metal-free ceramic crowns and single-sitting root canal treatment. Patients choosing a dentist search locally and decide quickly, so the clinic needed to appear credibly at that moment.

What we did

  • Built a clinic website that leads with treatments offered and practitioner credentials, the two things prospective patients check first
  • Optimised for local intent searches such as dentist in Panjim and dental clinic in North Goa
  • Built directory citations to strengthen local relevance for a single-location practice
  • Structured treatment information so each service could be found on its own terms

Why it worked

Healthcare decisions turn on trust. Foregrounding qualifications and specific treatments, then making the clinic easy to find in local search, addressed both halves of the problem. See the Kenkre Dental Care project page.

How We Approach Every Project

1. Research before execution

We start with the business goal, the competitive set and the way the client’s customers actually search. Keyword and intent research comes before any creative or media decision, which is why our SEO work and our advertising pull in the same direction.

2. Build the asset properly

Most campaigns fail on the destination, not the traffic. We build mobile-first, fast-loading sites through our web design practice, supported by logo and identity design and graphic design where a brand needs rebuilding from the ground up.

3. Amplify across the right channels

Depending on the objective that means paid search and social, public relations, influencer marketing, or film and television promotion. Channel follows objective, never the reverse.

4. Measure what matters

Every engagement gets goal tracking and analytics so spend can be tied to enquiries and revenue rather than impressions. See the full range of what we do in our competency overview.

How long does it take to see results from digital marketing?

Paid advertising can generate enquiries within days of launch. SEO is slower and compounding: meaningful movement in organic rankings typically takes three to six months depending on competition and the starting state of the website. We are explicit about this timeline before any engagement begins.

Do you only build websites, or do you run campaigns too?

Both, and they work best together. A website with no traffic and a campaign pointing at a poor website both waste budget. Our digital marketing services cover the full path from search visibility through to the page where the enquiry happens.
