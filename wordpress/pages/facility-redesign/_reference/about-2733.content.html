Meet the founder

The vision and discipline behind more than a decade of Sanctify's work.

PS

Punit Sahay

Founder & Proprietor Leading Sanctify since 2012

Punit Sahay founded Sanctify in 2012 with a clear conviction - that businesses in Goa deserved advertising and digital marketing built to a genuinely national standard. What began as a boutique studio in South Goa has grown, under his hands-on leadership, into one of the region's most trusted full-service agencies.

As Proprietor, Punit stays personally invested in the work, pairing a strategist's discipline with a creator's eye for detail. His philosophy is simple and unwavering: do great work, stay transparent, and treat every client's growth as your own.

More than a decade on, that same ethos continues to shape every brand, campaign and result Sanctify delivers.
