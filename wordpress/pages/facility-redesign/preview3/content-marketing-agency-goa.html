04 — FAQ

Questions, answered.

How is content marketing different from just blogging?
Blogging is one tactic; content marketing is a strategy. We map topics to search demand and buyer journeys, then interlink everything to build authority that compounds.
Do you write the content or just plan it?
Both — strategy, writing, editing and publishing are handled by our in-house team. All content is original and written for humans and search engines.
How does content help my rankings?
Well-structured content clusters signal topical authority to Google, support your SEO and earn links and trust — which lifts rankings across the site.
When will I see results?
Content is a compounding asset. Early pieces can rank within weeks for low-competition terms, while authority and traffic build meaningfully over three to six months.
